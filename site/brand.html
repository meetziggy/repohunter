RepoHunter

The visual + verbal system for RepoHunter — the tool that hunts down the best open-source so you reuse, don't reinvent. One rule: keep it sharp, generous, and unmistakably ours.

Essence

What we are

A hunter for code. RepoHunter scans millions of repos, finds the ones that fit your project and your machine, and hands you an honest dossier — adopt, adapt, or skip.

The symbol

The dragonfly — the most successful hunter on Earth (~95% strike rate). It doesn't chase; it predicts, intercepts, and connects. Precision over noise. That's the product.

Precise

Signal, not noise. Every claim earns its place.

Generous

Send the credit home. Promote authors, never strip them.

Relentless

Veteran grit. Keeps hunting so you don't have to.

Clear space & sizing

Keep breathing room equal to the dragonfly's head-height on all sides. Minimum legible size: 16px (the mark still reads as a favicon). Never crowd it with text or other marks.

On dark, always

The mark lives on near-black (#070b16/#0b1220) so the glow reads. On light backgrounds, use the badge (with its dark tile) — never the bare glowing mark.

✓Do use the badge on light photos; keep the wings symmetric; let it glow.
✓Pair with the wordmark set in the display font, cyan-tipped "Hunter".
✕Don't recolor to flat gray, stretch the wings, or add drop-shadows beyond the built-in glow.
✕Don't rotate, outline the letters, or place the bare mark on white.

Voice & Tone

We sound like a sharp friend who's done the homework — honest, a little blunt, never hype. We punch up at wasted effort and closed gardens, never down at people.

"Don't reinvent it. Someone already built it, and built it well — let's go get it, and send them the credit."
✓Plain English. Short sentences. Real verdicts (adopt / adapt / skip).
✓Credit maintainers by name. Gratitude over cleverness.
✕No "revolutionary", "synergy", "game-changer", or growth-hack slime.
✕Never trash a repo's author — critique code, respect people.
