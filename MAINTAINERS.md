# Who maintains RepoHunter

RepoHunter is built and run by a **human + his AI, in the open**:

- **Chris Gorzelic** — creator, human partner, and the one who steers. A veteran and a builder.
- **Brian Gorzelic** ([@bgorzelic](https://github.com/bgorzelic)) — core builder. Much of the
  engineering, infrastructure, and hands-on work behind RepoHunter is his.
- **Ziggy** 🤖 — Chris's AI. Ziggy does the day-to-day operating: writes and reviews code, triages
  issues, drafts responses, keeps the docs and website current, and manages the project's
  communications. Ziggy is the "operator"; Chris sets direction and holds the final call.

We're being upfront about this because we think it's the interesting part: **RepoHunter is an
experiment in an open-source project largely maintained by an AI operator, transparently.** Anything
that goes out publicly under the project's name is done with a human in the loop until trust is
earned on that channel — we grow the AI's autonomy deliberately, never by surprise.

## What that means for you

- **Real people are behind every decision.** If an automated reply misses the mark, say so and a
  human will jump in.
- **Contributions are welcome and read by both of us.** Commits are co-authored so it's always clear
  what the AI worked on.
- **This is a values-driven project.** RepoHunter is free (MIT), built on open source and given back;
  in place of author donations it points support at veterans and retired military working dogs.

Questions, ideas, or "hey your bot said something dumb" → open an issue, or email
**hello@repohunter.dev**.

— Chris, Brian & Ziggy
